## Full description

Laundry day should fit around your life, not take it over.

Dr Laundry helps you book laundry and garment care from your phone. Select your garments, confirm a pickup address and time, choose Standard or Express care, then follow your order through pickup, cleaning and delivery.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Book doorstep laundry pickup and delivery
• Choose individual garments and services with clear itemised prices
• Use your saved address or enter a different pickup address
• Select Standard service (estimated 72 hours) or Express service (estimated 24 hours)
• Review service charges and express fees before checkout
• Pay securely online; eligible first orders may use pay on delivery
• Follow live order status and, when available, driver journey progress
• View your order and payment history
• Receive useful pickup, payment, care and delivery notifications
• Manage your profile, preferences and support details

CLEAR FROM SELECTION TO DELIVERY

Before placing an order, review your selected garments, quantities, service speed, pickup details and complete price. After placing it, your order history keeps its latest status and estimated delivery details together.

UPDATES THAT MATTER

Follow each stage of your order from collection to delivery. Notifications help you stay informed when an order is picked up, its status changes or it is ready for the next step.

STANDARD OR EXPRESS

Choose the turnaround that suits your schedule. Standard orders have an estimated 72-hour turnaround. Express orders have an estimated 24-hour turnaround, with the applicable express fee shown before payment.

PAYMENT VISIBILITY

See whether an order is paid or unpaid and review past transactions in payment history. Online payment details are handled securely by our payment provider; Dr Laundry does not store your full card number.

SUPPORT AND ACCOUNT CONTROL

Update your personal details, manage settings and contact support when you need help. You can also request account deletion from the app or through the Dr Laundry website.

Service availability, pickup windows and delivery estimates may vary based on order details and operating conditions. Dr Laundry is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
