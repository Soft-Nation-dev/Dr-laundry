# Store artwork generation prompts

These are the final prompts used to create the source artwork. The exported Play-ready files were resized and colour-formatted without changing the composition.

## App icon prompt

Create a production-ready Google Play app icon for the existing brand Dr Laundry, using the attached current logo only as brand reference.

Format/composition: square 1:1 artwork, full bleed to every edge. Do not add rounded corners, an outer device frame, a drop shadow outside the artwork or transparent padding; Google Play applies its own mask. Use one centered, instantly readable emblem with generous internal breathing room and a strong silhouette that remains clear at 48 px. Include no words, letters, tagline, price, rating badge, notification dot, Google Play symbol or tiny details.

Preserve the recognizable idea of the existing logo: premium garment care represented by a laundry basket with neatly arranged fabric or garments, subtly elevated with a refined care-service seal. Simplify the detailed current logo into a mature luxury mark. Use a deep royal plum-to-indigo background, warm ivory circular seal, antique-gold basket outline and restrained teal and burgundy fabric accents. The result should feel premium, vintage-inspired, trustworthy and polished, with crisp vector-like geometry, subtle dimensional lighting, tactile enamel texture, high contrast and clean edges. The medical or doctor cue should be extremely subtle or omitted if it harms clarity; the laundry basket must be the primary read. Do not use a mockup, phone, extra objects, photography or watermark.

## Feature graphic prompt

Create a premium landscape Google Play feature-graphic master artwork for Dr Laundry, using the current logo and newly simplified icon only as palette and brand references.

Use a wide cinematic banner approximately 2.048:1, designed to be center-cropped and exported at exactly 1024 × 500 px. Keep all important subjects within the central 70% safe area; edges may contain only background texture or decorative fabric folds. Do not include device mockups, app screenshots, a repeated icon badge, words, letters, tagline, price, rating, store badges or watermark.

Convey premium doorstep laundry and garment care in one elegant visual: a beautifully folded ivory shirt, deep-plum garment and teal accent fabric arranged with a refined gold clothes hanger and a subtle clean-laundry basket silhouette. Add understated cues for freshness and reliable movement through a soft ribbon-like sweep, delicate sparkle highlights and a faint route arc, but no map pins or UI. Use a deep royal plum-to-indigo gradient background, antique-gold details, warm ivory textiles and restrained burgundy and teal accents. Aim for premium vintage editorial advertising, expensive textile textures, soft directional light, tasteful depth, high contrast and one clear focal arrangement. Avoid clutter, fine detail, people, medical imagery and pure black, white or dark gray as the dominant background.
