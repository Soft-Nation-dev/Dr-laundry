Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Dr Laundry collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Effective and last updated: 29 August 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to customers, drivers, administrators and other people who use Dr Laundry. By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Name, email address, phone number and authentication information.
  • Saved pickup or delivery addresses and an optional profile photo.
  • Your account role and account preferences.

Order and payment information

  • Selected laundry items, service type, quantities, pickup window, delivery details, order status and instructions.
  • Payment amount, status, provider reference and transaction audit details. Dr Laundry does not receive or store your full payment-card number; payment processing is handled by the payment provider.

Location information

  • Customer location or address coordinates used to suggest and confirm pickup and delivery locations.
  • Driver foreground or background location during an active job, used to support assignment, routing, live tracking, pickup and delivery.

Device, notification and support information

  • Device push-notification tokens and notification preferences.
  • Technical information such as app errors and basic operational logs needed for security and reliability.
  • Messages and details you provide when contacting support.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, verify identity and provide role-appropriate access.
  • Schedule, price, fulfil, track and support laundry orders.
  • Process payments, show payment history and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Provide address suggestions, route drivers and show relevant live tracking.
  • Send account, order, payment, pickup, delivery and service notifications.
  • Respond to support requests, resolve disputes and improve service reliability.
  • Comply with law, enforce our terms and protect customers, drivers and Dr Laundry.

4. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide and protect the service. This may include:

  • Service personnel: relevant order, contact and location details may be visible to authorised drivers and administrators.
  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase supports authentication, database and realtime features; Cloudflare supports application infrastructure and image delivery.
  • Payment providers: Paystack processes payments and returns transaction status and references.
  • Maps and location providers: Google Maps Platform services support address search, geocoding, maps and routes.
  • Notification providers: Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging support push notifications.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent harm, fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Permissions used by the app

Depending on your role and chosen features, the app may request location, background location, notifications, camera or photo-library access. Location access is used for pickup, delivery, routing and live job tracking. Camera or photo access is used only when you choose to upload a profile image. You can manage permissions in Android settings, though disabling one may prevent the related feature from working.

Active driver journeys: Dr Laundry collects precise location data to enable live pickup and delivery journey tracking for the assigned customer, even when the app is closed or not in use. A driver starts sharing from an accepted task, Android displays a persistent notification while background sharing is active, and sharing stops when the driver completes or manually stops the journey.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use access controls, encrypted connections and role-based permissions designed to limit unauthorised access. No system can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong password and protect your device.

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above. Account profile data can be deleted following a verified request. Certain order, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute and audit records may be retained where reasonably required for legal, accounting, security or legitimate business obligations. When practical, retained records are restricted or de-identified.

7. Your choices and rights

You may update eligible profile information in the app, manage device permissions and notification preferences, or request access, correction or deletion by contacting us. To initiate account deletion outside the app, visit our account deletion page.

8. Children’s privacy

Dr Laundry is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly permit minors to create accounts or place service orders. If you believe a minor has provided information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, we rely on appropriate provider safeguards and use the information only for the purposes described in this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our service, providers or legal obligations. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email admin@drlaundry.com.ng or visit Dr Laundry Support.
