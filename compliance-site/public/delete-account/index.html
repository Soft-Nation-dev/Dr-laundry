Account control

Delete your Dr Laundry account

You can start deletion from this page even if you no longer have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address registered to your Dr Laundry account.
    Use the button below; it prepares the correct subject line.
  2. Include your registered phone number.
    You may include a recent order reference to help us locate and verify the correct account. Never send your password or card details.
  3. Complete identity verification if requested.
    We use verification to prevent another person from deleting your account without permission.
Email deletion requestContact support

Processing time: After verification, we aim to complete eligible deletion requests within 30 days. We will contact you if an active order, unresolved payment or legal obligation requires additional steps.

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile and eligible personal contact information.
  • Saved addresses, notification tokens and account preferences.
  • Profile images and active location data where they are no longer needed for an active order, safety or a legal obligation.

What may be retained

Some order, payment, refund, fraud-prevention, dispute and audit records may be kept for legal, accounting, security or legitimate business obligations. Access to retained information is restricted and, where practical, it is de-identified. Retained data is deleted or anonymised when the applicable purpose or retention period ends.

What happens next

When deletion is complete, you will lose access to the account, order history and associated app features. Account deletion cannot be undone. If you only need help signing in or correcting profile information, contact support instead.
